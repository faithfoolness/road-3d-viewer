Mouse
  • Left button: drag the scene
  • Right button: orbit around the point under the cursor
  • Wheel: zoom towards the cursor
  • Double click: fly closer to the point
  • Double click on a layer: zoom to the whole layer
Panels and switches
  • Layers (left): visibility of models and DXF layers
  • Lighting (right): daylight or studio; the slider sets brightness
  • / : low-power mode for slower PCs (simpler rendering while moving, full quality once stopped) or full performance
  • ☀ / ☾: light or dark scene background
Top bar
  • Open file: IFC, OBJ, LAS/LAZ, DXF
  • Top view: the whole plan, north up
Measuring
  • Measure: distance between two points on the model. Below it: L: horizontal distance, H: height difference, i: slope, %
  • Lane width: straight across the lane, between the centre lines of the markings; points snap to the marking's centre line
  • Height: plumb line from a point on an object down to the surface below
  • Coordinates: latitude and longitude of a point (WGS 84); the button next to it copies them; the UTM zone of the models is set in the chip below the buttons
  • Clear: remove all measurements
  • Right click on a measurement: delete just that one
  • Esc: cancel the measurement in progress
